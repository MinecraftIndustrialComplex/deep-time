Three things go wrong at your volcano

  1. 1.The projection squashes a round cone into a slab

    At this spot Petroff-Guyou draws a block as 447 m east-west and 194 m north-south: 2.3 to 1 here, up to 2.67 on the planet. The round cone becomes an 89 x 206-block slab running north-south. From the east or west it is a knife edge.

  2. 2.The vent is not the summit

    The cone is added on top of the seaward slope of a narrow coastal range. The ground under the vent already tilts about 2 blocks per block, as steep as the cone near the vent. So the highest point is the range's crest 13 to 27 blocks away, and the 3-block crater vanishes into the tilt.

  3. 3.The paint is Terralith's, not ours

    • Black stripes are contours of a 2D noise (where |n| < 0.11 in Terralith's volcano_surface), not lava flows.
    • Orange specks are Terralith's crater-only magma rule firing on a slope. Our crater zone is an 8.75-block disc at the catalogue centre, which sits on the slope.
    • White patches are vanilla's frozen peaks, at an 11 C mean, 2.3 degrees from the equator.
    • The terracotta patch is probably Terralith's hot-spring biome (Yellowstone), in discs about 25 blocks (7 km) in radius. Partly unverified.

World gen in general: a design gap, not realism

Our terrain is a pure heightfield with almost no small detail and no 3D noise. Real gentle slopes of 5 to 15 degrees become smooth stair-steps of 45 to 66 degrees. Terralith and Tectonic break every face with 3D noise and jagged ridges.
