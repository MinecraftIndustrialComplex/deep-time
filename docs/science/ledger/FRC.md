# Ledger FRC: Plate forces (the force balance that moves the plates)

Part of the claims ledger ([claims.md](../claims.md): how to read the columns and the status marks). Feature page: [plate-forces.md](../plate-forces.md).

Rows: 30. By status: `verified` 3, `from-memory` 10, `abstract-only` 3, `game-choice` 14.

Added 2026-10-02 with task 5 of `docs/design/plate-force-balance.md` (the force balance computed and recorded each step, not applied to the plates), by the agent that wrote the code. Since task 7 (the switch, 2026-10-04) the balance moves the plates. The settings of the balance are ledgered as PLT-181 to PLT-191 (rewritten the same day to describe the code); the rows here are the constants and laws inside `crates/deeptime-core/src/plates/forces.rs` and the editor hints of the new settings. Sources were read in the project's source library on the test VM (`/Volumes/Graphide/ban/deeptime-sources/<Key>/<Key>.txt`, text extractions of the PDFs) by a Sonnet sub-agent on 2026-10-02, which quoted each passage with its line number; "read" below means that. Turcotte & Schubert, Parsons & Richter 1980, Molnar & Lyon-Caen 1988, Schellart 2004, Carlson et al. 1983, van Hunen & Allen 2011, Cloos 1993 and Coblentz et al. 1994 are not in the library (checked the same day), so every value that rests on them is `from-memory`. Paths are relative to the repository root; line numbers are left out on purpose.
