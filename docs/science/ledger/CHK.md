# Ledger CHK: plate-check target ranges

Part of the claims ledger ([claims.md](../claims.md): how to read the columns and the status marks).

Rows: 59. By status: `verified` 19, `cited-unverified` 3, `from-memory` 1, `abstract-only` 7, `game-choice` 11, `uncited` 18 (after WP-13a, 2026-10-02, which read the Earth figures behind the force-balance targets and changed two ranges, CHK-020 and CHK-036; CHK-058 and CHK-059 were added the same day with the force balance's rows, task 5, whose reported measures carry no range and so no row; CHK-020 and CHK-028 noted with the speed scale at 75, 2026-10-26, no status changed).

Added in stage 2, package WP-0 (2026-10-02), by reading the code as it stood on master 1aa0579 (stage 1 had not
covered it). Nothing here has been checked against its source. Paths are relative to the repository root; line
numbers are left out on purpose.

Scope: the target ranges and thresholds of `deeptime plate-check` in `crates/deeptime-core/src/plates/check.rs` (the `MEASURES` table, the `Params` defaults and the hard-coded thresholds inside the measure functions), read against `docs/design/plate-check-baseline.md` and `docs/design/plate-force-balance.md` (sections 3.2, 3.5, 6, 7 and 12), plus `docs/design/plate-collisions-and-welding.md` section 6 where `check.rs` names it as the source of the ranges. Rows CHK-001 to CHK-019 are the 19 ranges of the collisions design, CHK-020 to CHK-036 the 17 added by the force-balance design (task 1b), CHK-037 to CHK-040 the four ranges that wait for physics, CHK-041 to CHK-057 the definitions and thresholds the ranges are judged with. The ranges of the first group are already ledgered as PLT-143 to PLT-161 (the range lives in `check.rs`, so each has its own CHK row that names the PLT row; the status mark follows the PLT row). Already ledgered and not repeated: PLT-163 (slab-attached when slab share >= 0.2, continental when share >= 0.5, in `measures_with`), PLT-164 to PLT-167 (the margin definitions in `stats.rs`), PLT-168 to PLT-173 (the hypsometry targets, which are not in `check.rs`), PLT-022 and PLT-195 (the speed cap and its planned raise to 250). Left out on purpose: the reported-only measures (no range, so no claim; their labels carry no Earth figure except `speed_slab_ratio`, which has a row), array sizes and other engineering constants (`PIECES_KEPT` 16, hop limit 100000, tolerances 1e-6), the quantile and rank-correlation arithmetic, the rendering code, the test modules (none restates an Earth figure), and the "Now" and baseline figures of the design documents (they are measurements of the model, not claims about the Earth). `pending-rewrite` is used only on rows whose measured quantity comes from code the force-balance design says is replaced (section 6: `Pull`, the front length); it is not used on the ranges themselves, because the force-balance design (section 7) says the collisions table "stands" and adds to it: the check is the acceptance test of the rewrite, not something the rewrite replaces (see the flag on PLT-143 to PLT-161).

## Flags

- claims.md fix 18 (CHK-056): "Earth: 3 to 4" for slab-attached plates over the others is written in `check.rs` without an author and in `plate-check-baseline.md` as "(Forsyth and Uyeda)". The force-balance design attributes the same "about four times faster" to Conrad and Lithgow-Bertelloni 2002 and 2004 (section 3.2) and uses it as the Earth column of the 2.5 target in CHK-025. The target row CHK-025 itself names no author.
- PLT-143 to PLT-161 are all marked `pending-rewrite`, but the force-balance design (section 7) says the collisions design's table "stands" and adds to it, and does not list the check among the things it replaces (section 6). I left the mark off the CHK range rows (CHK-001 to CHK-040) and put it only on CHK-050, CHK-052 and CHK-056, whose quantity comes from the `Pull` front length that section 6 replaces.
- Hard-coded values that ignore the parameter they resemble (CHK-006, CHK-007, CHK-009, CHK-018, CHK-049): `rift_alive_50_pct` and `rift_welded_back_50_pct` use a literal 50.0, so `--param rift_window_myr` moves only the mega-plate rift measures; the land gain uses a literal 10.0 instead of `start_up_myr`; the active margin mean uses 90.0 although its label says 100 Myr (also noted in PLT-160).
- CHK-019 and CHK-055: the 230 Myr ceiling in the collisions design (section 6) sits next to "208-269 Myr", the last-step value; the check applies it to the run mean of the per-step 90th percentile (mean 200 Myr on the baseline). The same ceiling therefore judges a different quantity than the one the design wrote it for; PLT-161 records the end-of-run reading.
- CHK-021: the target "at most 20 mm/yr" for the 10th percentile is below the Earth figure the design gives beside it (slowest group 26-28 mm/yr), and the Earth figure is for a group of plates, not a percentile of plate-steps.
- CHK-036: the period target (300-600 Myr) extends above the Earth range printed in the same design row (300-500); the tenure target (CHK-035) is identical to its Earth range. Both "Now" figures (360 and 650 Myr) come from one run under no stated definition (baseline document), so the design's Now column is not comparable with the definition coded in CHK-041/CHK-042.
- CHK-037: the target ratio is defined at 20 Myr after onset; the Earth figure quoted (0.27) is "in 15 Myr", and 0.27 equals 40/150 of the India figures in section 3.2, which carry no time.
- CHK-039, CHK-040: the design restricts these to "a belt above 3 km"; neither the label nor the measure carries that condition. **Settled in plate tasks 3 and 4 (2026-10-02):** the record keeps the foredeep only for belts that reached 3 km, and the labels say so.
- CHK-053: the design's "0.5-2 % of plate-steps turning about a pole near themselves" has no definition; the coded 30 degrees was the check's reading, and the baseline measures 6.3 % (pole within 30 degrees) and 10.9 % (pole anywhere on the plate).
- CHK-026 and CHK-027 name Forsyth & Uyeda as the source of the Earth cell without giving any number; section 12 of the design says most of its papers were read from abstracts or secondary pages. The "factor of 8" for continental drag attributed to Forsyth & Uyeda (design section 3.2) is not a CHK row; see claims.md.
