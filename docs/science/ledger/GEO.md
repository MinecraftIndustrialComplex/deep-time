# Ledger GEO: Geothermal features

Part of the claims ledger ([claims.md](../claims.md): how to read the columns and the status marks). Feature page: [geothermal-features.md](../geothermal-features.md).

Rows: 51. By status: `game-choice` 40, `uncited` 11.

Added in stage 2, package WP-0 (2026-10-02), by reading the code as it stood on master e057a04, the merge of P2-14 (stage 1
ran before the feature existed). Nothing here has been checked against its source. Paths are relative to the repository root; line
numbers are left out on purpose.

Scope: the geothermal features (hot springs with sinter or travertine terraces, fumaroles with native sulfur and altered ground) that the merge of the P2-14 branch added to master, placed around the active volcanoes. Read in full: the four `geothermal_*` parameters and their defaults and ranges in `crates/deeptime-core/src/underground.rs`; the same four keys in the eight presets under `crates/deeptime-core/presets/` (all eight carry the same values); the four field hints and the section hint in `crates/deeptime-viewer/assets/editor_fields.json`; the section "Geothermal features (P2-14) and their settings" of `crates/deeptime-format/FORMAT.md`; `Geothermal.java`, `GeothermalPalette.java`, `GeothermalRules.java`, `GeothermalEnv.java` and `GeothermalPass.java` under `neoforge/src/main/java/com/minecraftindustrialcomplex/deeptime/worldgen/`, the geothermal call in `GeologicChunkGenerator.java` and the warm-ground method `geothermalWarmthC` in `neoforge/src/main/java/com/minecraftindustrialcomplex/deeptime/api/climate/DeepTimeClimate.java`; the P2-14 text in `STATUS.md` (status-table row, the two Pending questions, and the surviving bullets of the build-log entry), in `docs/deeptime-implementation-plan.md` (section 10, P2-14 and its "As built" paragraph) and in `docs/deeptime-design-decisions.md` (the entries of 2026-10-01 that accept the feature and allow it to start). Left out on purpose, as engineering and not claims: the tests (`GeothermalTest`, `GeothermalGameTests`, the edited `AdvancedSettingsTest` and the editor test count in `crates/deeptime-cli/src/watch/editor.rs`), `tools/workbench/geothermal_stats.py` and `tools/workbench/preview.py`, the probe command (`GeothermalProbe.java`, `DeepTimeCommands.java`), the lang labels in `en_us.json` (field and section titles such as "Rain needed for hot springs", no figure), the slider limits in the editor file (they restate the validation ranges of GEO-002, GEO-004, GEO-006 and GEO-008), hashing and seeds, the priority order between parts, the reach and hard-radius arithmetic (the 1.34 outline factor), the carving masks, the support fill under rims and beds, the water-safety rules, the tag lookup of the block palette, the 16x16 chunk loop, the climate API version number and the measured results of the build (260 sites, 23 pools and so on, which are outputs of one run and not claims). Nothing here says how many geyser fields or hot-spring areas Earth has, what temperature a vent or spring has, or that a spring boils: the code has no geysers, no temperatures and no boiling rule (see GEO-026 and the Flags). Already ledgered, so no new row: the conductive geotherm used for burial and metamorphism (RCK-028, RCK-030, RCK-031, ERO-037), the plate model's heat flow (PLT-137, PLT-138), the geothermal flux in the sea-ice comment (CLM-097), the volcano list and activity that the sites hang on (VOL-054), the chamber depth and crater-lake settings of the same `underground` section (MAG-002, MAG-019, MAG-034, HNT-081) and the travertine rock class (ERO-126). Abbreviations in the "citation written in" column: "build log P2-14" means the surviving bullets of the 2026-10-01 P2-14 entry in `STATUS.md` (the entry's heading and first paragraphs were lost in the merge, see the Flags); "STATUS P2-14" means its status-table row.

## Flags

- GEO-014 and GEO-048: the settings hint (and the code comment on `geothermal_density`) says the default is "one to four" fields per volcano. The coded rule floor(d x (0.7 + 2.3 a) x clamp(sqrt(R/45), 0.6, 2.2) + u) gives 0 or 1 at the smallest, least active volcano (a = 0.05, expected 0.49) and 6 or 7 at the largest, most active (a = 1, R of 218 blocks or more, expected 6.6). The build log measures 260 sites around 40 of 67 volcanoes, so some volcanoes get none.
- GEO-049: "The first field is always near the summit" holds at the default reach only; the first field is 0.08 to 0.35 radii times the reach from the summit (up to 1.05 radii at reach 3).
- GEO-050: "2 to 5 blocks across the middle" does not agree with the code, where 2 to 5 is the pool's mean radius (GEO-027); across, a main pool is about 4 to 10 blocks, before the outline wobble of GEO-032.
- GEO-035: FORMAT.md says a tenth of the apron is stained yellow; `Geothermal.java` stains a cell when its hash unit exceeds 0.93, which is 7 percent.
- GEO-017: FORMAT.md says a field must be drawn on ground 3 blocks above the sea; the field centre is tested against sea level + 3 + 2 (5 blocks), and the 3 applies to each site's columns.
- GEO-046: FORMAT.md says the warm-ground signal reaches "within 10 blocks under and 6 over its ground"; for pools it is 12 under.
- GEO-020: the code comment says a mean under 3 degC "freezes standing water"; the build log says the real reason is vanilla's top-layer freezing. A hot spring in ground with a 3 degC annual mean does not freeze, so the threshold is a game rule (the row is `game-choice`), but the comment reads as physics.
- GEO-012 and GEO-013: the plan (accepted text, GEO-011) says the features come "from the heat flow already modelled"; the code does not use it, the bake does not export it, and Earth's spreading ridges, rifts and hotspots get nothing. The plan's "As built" paragraph records this; the open question in STATUS Pending has not been put to Ben.
- GEO-026: the scope given for this ledger named geysers; no code, hint, plan text or decisions-log entry has a geyser (the accepted feature of GEO-011 does not name them), and there is no boiling, steam, eruption or temperature rule. The old stub `docs/science/geothermal-features.md` still says "hot springs, geysers and fumaroles".
- GEO-041: the plan and the code comment tie cinnabar to epithermal systems; the code makes it a 6 percent stain on altered ground at silicic volcanoes (GEO-040) and says there is no deposit behind it. The geologist's remark quoted in `docs/research/notes/geologist_youtuber_feedback.md` (about acid sulfur caves, not about springs) says wall minerals there are gypsum rather than sulfur or cinnabar; it is not cited in the code.
- GEO-007 and GEO-019: the 400 mm threshold and the rule "springs need that much rain" have no source or reason anywhere. The Java class comment says springs fail "where the mean temperature is near freezing" while the constant is 3 degC (GEO-020).
- The build-log entry for P2-14 in `STATUS.md` is damaged: its heading and first paragraphs are missing, and its surviving bullets (gates, "Also seen, not changed", "Contradicts the plan", "Next step") sit at the end of the P1-10 TerraBlender entry. The P2-14 status-table row and the two Pending questions are intact.
