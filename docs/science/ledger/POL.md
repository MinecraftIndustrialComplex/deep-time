# Ledger POL: The pole cap

The pole cap went with the playable Lambert world on 2026-10-24 (cube plan §7.4; Ben, decisions log 2026-10-24: "Cube only (Recommended)"): POL-001 to POL-015 and the Petroff-Guyou border's POL-022 are retired (`merged`, their text kept after "Was:"). The cube world's temporary border is POL-023, POL-024 and POL-031.

Part of the claims ledger ([claims.md](../claims.md): how to read the columns and the status marks). Feature page: [pole-cap.md](../pole-cap.md). Extraction notes with flags: [extraction-notes/SCL.md](../extraction-notes/SCL.md).

Rows: 9 (16 further rows are `merged` and not counted: POL-001 to POL-015 and POL-022, retired 2026-10-24 with the pole cap and the Petroff-Guyou world). By status: `game-choice` 8, `uncited` 1 (recounted 2026-10-24 with `tools/review/claims_counts.py`).

Extracted from the code as it stood on 2026-10-01/02 by a stage-1 agent. Nothing here has been checked against its source. Paths are relative to the repository root; line numbers are left out on purpose.

Java files are under `neoforge/src/main/java/com/minecraftindustrialcomplex/deeptime/`. The simulation covers the whole sphere pole to pole; the pole cap is a gameplay barrier on the server, it does not change the terrain. "Decisions file" is `docs/deeptime-design-decisions.md`. Curve shapes and the player policy were decided by the agent in the P1-18 build log (2026-09-28), inside Ben's 2026-09-27 answer "a soft cap at about +-75 to 80 degrees (pushback and storms) first".
