# Ledger PRS: Presets other than earthlike_quick_16k, and the test presets

Part of the claims ledger ([claims.md](../claims.md): how to read the columns and the status marks).

Rows: 74. By status: `uncited` 9, `cited-unverified` 9, `calibrated` 2, `game-choice` 54 (each row counted once by its first mark)

Added in stage 2, package WP-0 (2026-10-02), by reading the code as it stood on master 1aa0579 (stage 1 had not
covered it). Nothing here has been checked against its source. Paths are relative to the repository root; line
numbers are left out on purpose.

Scope. (1) The three other earthlike presets (`earthlike_quick_32k`, `earthlike_full_16k`, `earthlike_full_32k`), the four `pipeline_*` presets and the four `plates_*` presets under `crates/deeptime-core/presets/`, diffed key by key against `earthlike_quick_16k`. The three other earthlike presets and the four `pipeline_*` presets differ from `earthlike_quick_16k` only in `name`, `description`, `scale.circumference_blocks`, `resolution.*` and (the two full presets) two plate-uplift keys; the `pipeline_*` presets are identical to the `earthlike_*` of the same size in every key except `name` and `description`. The `plates_*` presets are a different, much smaller file (126 lines; only `planet`, `scale`, `keyframes`, `resolution`, `tectonics`) and differ from `earthlike_quick_16k` in 16 claims, which are rows here. (2) The six test presets under `crates/deeptime-core/tests/presets/`: `planet_world_4k.json` (a full planet preset, diffed against `earthlike_quick_16k`), the four `deposits_*` presets and `dummy.json` (different formats: a `deposits_synthetic` or `dummy` section, not a planet preset). (3) The in-game world presets `neoforge/src/main/resources/data/deeptime/worldgen/world_preset/planet.json` and `planet_32k.json`, the noise settings `worldgen/noise_settings/planet.json` and the dimension type `dimension_type/planet_overworld.json` they point to.

Already ledgered, so no new row (same value, same place): the circumference, resolution and L0 sample counts of the four earthlike presets (SCL-004, SCL-042, SCL-045); the uplift values 0.42, 0.34, 1400 and 1100 as such (HYP-019, PLT-039, PLT-073; PRS-002 adds the reasoning); the pole-cap latitude 75 and stop 80 in `planet.json` (POL-003, POL-004; claims.md fix 19: the Rust `scale.pole_cap_deg` is read by nothing); the build range -352 to 639, height 992 and sea level 63 in the dimension type and noise settings (SCL-006 to SCL-009); the impact flux 40 and largest crater 40 km in `planet_world_4k.json` (IMP-139, IMP-006); the Nether loop at 1:8 (POL-016).

Left out on purpose, and why. Engineering values, not claims: seeds, keyframe picture sizes, `dummy.json` step counts, sleep, stage names, event and site counts (`steps`, `sleep_ms`, `stages`, `event_every_steps`, `sites`), `plates_*` `tectonics.l1_every_steps` (0 against 1; the sampling schedule of the L1 raster, which validation requires to be 1 once a surface stage exists), the `schema` and `name` keys, the descriptions as text (except where they state a claim), the `noise.size_horizontal` and `size_vertical` cell sizes, `disable_mob_generation`, `legacy_random_source`, the biome source, the End and Nether entries of the world preset (copied from vanilla's `normal` preset), the whole vanilla-derived `final_density` graph and its constants (0.64, 0.1171875, 0.078125, 1.5625, 4.0, 0.27, 1.5 and so on), and the noise-settings file `planet_nocaves.json`, which no world preset points to (it differs from `planet.json` in `aquifers_enabled` false and in the cave branch of `final_density`; used by GameTest dimensions). Keys that `planet_world_4k.json` leaves out take their Rust defaults; those defaults were compared with the `earthlike_quick_16k` values for the climate carbon-cycle and ice keys, `surface.glacial`, `hydro`, `biomes`, `underground`, `amplify`, `structure.deformation` and the deposit multipliers, and they are equal (PRS-045). The numbers inside the invented test history of `crates/deeptime-core/src/deposits/synthetic.rs` (continent positions, event dates) are code, not presets, and are not read here.
