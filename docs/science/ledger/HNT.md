Part of the claims ledger ([claims.md](../claims.md): how to read the columns and the status marks).

WP-15 (2026-10-02) changed the hint texts that disagreed with the code or the sources (claims.md fixes 27 to 32 and the geothermal hints, GEO-047 to GEO-051), kept the hint text in one place (`crates/deeptime-viewer/assets/editor_fields.json`, which the browser workbench and the in-game editor both read; the lang file holds only labels and tooltips without figures, except HNT-084), and recorded in each row what was changed under "before WP-15" in the status cell. The earlier statuses are kept in square brackets. Code was not changed except comments (the "15 times taller than wide" wording and the geothermal comments), so no GameTest was needed. Hints in other work packages' areas (plate, impact, volcano, deposit, climate values) were corrected only where the hint contradicted the code or a source already read; the values behind them stay with their packages (WP-1, WP-2, WP-5, WP-9, WP-13, WP-14).

Added in stage 2, package WP-0 (2026-10-02), by reading the code as it stood on master 1aa0579 (stage 1 had not
covered it). Nothing here has been checked against its source. Paths are relative to the repository root; line
numbers are left out on purpose.

Scope: the hint, label and tooltip texts that the world editor shows players and that state a physical fact, an Earth figure, a range of Earth values or a rule. Read in full: `crates/deeptime-viewer/assets/editor_fields.json` (186 field entries and the section texts), the labels and tooltips in `neoforge/src/main/resources/assets/deeptime/lang/en_us.json`, and the player-facing strings of the other viewer assets (`workbench.js`, `index.html`, `viewer.js`, `worldmap.js`, `deposits.js`); nothing in them states an Earth figure beyond the preset names "Earth-like". The Java side holds no hint text with a figure (a search for `Component.literal` strings with digits found none). Each row quotes the claim-bearing sentence of a hint exactly (the quote is an exact piece of the hint, not always the whole hint) and gives the slider range the field offers; `source cited` is only what the hint names, and `citation written in` says what a code comment, build-log entry or document names for the same figure. Where a coded figure is cited in code or in an existing ledger row but not in the hint, the status follows that row and the cell says so. Hints were added to the catalogue in build log 2026-09-29 P1-20 ("every field gained a one-line hint") and with each later setting; no log entry records a source for any hint figure.

Already ledgered, so no new row (same hint at the same place): PLT-203 (plate_count), PLT-204 (ridge_depth, abyssal_depth), PLT-205 (shelf_share), PLT-206 (mountain_belt_width, arc_range_width, arc_range_height), PLT-207 (subduction_start_age), PLT-208 (crust_strength), HYD-061 (lake_min_area), HYD-065 (lake_evaporation_base), IMP-016 (impact_max_crater), IMP-022 (impactor_density), IMP-071 (impact_sea_cutoff), IMP-083 (crater_diffusivity), SCL-020 (slider ranges of m_per_block_h and m_per_block_v). The impact_flux hint is also already IMP-018; it has a row here because the task asked for its flag to be recorded (the hint is not changed). New observations on these existing rows are in the Flags.

Left out on purpose: (1) hints that only restate a default; all were compared with the eight presets and agree (build_limit_band 48, glacial_width 3, breakup_rate 0.012, wetland_flat_share 0.35, wetland_lowland 150 m, inland_mid and inland_far 200 and 1000 blocks, river_min_area 50, river_max_width 64, river_max_depth 12, river_freeboard 1, river_smoothing 8, lake_min_depth 1, playa_blocks 2, river_biome_width 6, bank_zone 1, bank_ratio 0.5, strata_minor_rock 0.15, crater_lakes 0.25); their values are the game-choice rows of the other ledgers (HYD, BIO, STR, MAG); (2) hints with no figure and no physical rule (seed, section descriptions, the fantasy toggles, essentials, beach_width, terrain_biome_scale, variant_scale, province_size, brine_reach and the other pure game settings), and slider ranges and counts that are engineering limits; (3) lang labels, event names ("Snowball Earth" and the like), the memory-cap, timeline and share-code texts, and the pole-cap warnings, none of which states a figure (the pole cap is ledgered in POL); (4) hints that only name a place without a figure (Andes-style, Himalaya-style). ## Flags

WP-15 (2026-10-02) corrected the hints behind the flags for HNT-002, 005, 006, 015, 016, 017, 018, 019, 022, 027, 031, 034, 035, 054, 057, 059, 062, 063, 071, 073, 074, 075 and 077 (see each row's status cell and the geothermal rows GEO-047 to GEO-051). Still open: HNT-021 (the richness setting reaches no block, WP-9), the arc-spacing and caldera design ranges (WP-2), the plate hints PLT-204 to PLT-208 (WP-13: the "Abyssal plain depth" label sets `oceanic_floor_elevation_m`, not the unused `abyssal_elevation_m`; the 180 Myr figure is used for two different things) and the hint of `lapse_rate`, `initial_co2` and `star_age` whose Earth figures were seen only in search summaries. The flag text below is the WP-0 text and describes the state before WP-15.
- HNT-018 (impact_flux): KNOWN WRONG by a factor of a million. The hint says one crater of 20 km or more per 180 million km² per year; the coded rate (IMP-001) is 5.6e-15 per km² per year, one per 1.8e14 km² per year (IMP-018, impacts.md, claims.md flagged row 9, STATUS.md stage-1 summary item 8). The hint was not changed.
- HNT-017 and HNT-019 (volcano_flanks, crater_depth): the hints, and the params.rs comment behind VOL-024, say "blocks are 15 times taller than wide". The scale is 300 m across and 20 m up per block, so 300 / 20 = 15 means a block spans 15 times more ground than height and slopes are drawn 15 times steeper (SCL-003). The sentence says the opposite. **HNT-017 and the params.rs comment fixed in WP-2 (2026-10-02);** HNT-019 (impacts) is left to its package.
- HNT-057 (ice_sheet_height): the hint says "About 50 kPa matches Antarctica", but the ice.rs comment and CLM-102 give Antarctica about 32 kPa and Greenland about 80 kPa, with 50 kPa chosen between them to meet a target.
- HNT-062 and HNT-063 (calderas): the hint calls Crater Lake's radius "about 4 km" while the volcanoes/params.rs comment gives 4 to 5 km across; the preset's largest caldera radius is 9 km against the hint's 20 km for Yellowstone. **Fixed in WP-2 (2026-10-02):** the hints now give read Earth figures; see the rows.
- HNT-016 and HNT-059 (shields): volcano_size says a shield island stands "up to 6 km above the sea floor", shield_height_high says "Hawaii: 5–9 km", the coded range is 3.5 to 6.5 km and its comment says 5 to 9 km (VOL-021). **Fixed in WP-2 (2026-10-02):** the hints now give read Earth figures; see the rows.
- HNT-015 and HNT-058 (volcano spacing): the hints give "Earth's spacing" and "real arcs: 50–70 km", but the one paper the code cites for it says in its abstract that arc spacing has no characteristic value (STATUS.md stage-1 summary item 9; VOL-039). **Fixed in WP-2 (2026-10-02):** the hints now give read Earth figures; see the rows.
- HNT-022 and HNT-021 (deposits): feature_deposits says 1 is the true size at the block scale, but every deposit type enlarges its body 6 to 12 times horizontally (DEP-195, DEP-198); deposit_richness promises richer ore though the richness setting does not reach the blocks (STATUS.md stage-1 item 3, DEP-203).
- HNT-054 (star_age): "A younger star is fainter" is not what the model does: the Gough law is normalised to 1 at the end of the history for any star age (only the brightening rate and the earlier past change).
- HNT-071 (ejecta_reach): the hint says the blanket is 1/27 of the rim thickness at 3 radii; the coded profile (IMP-055) has a taper that makes it zero at 3 radii.
- HNT-005 and HNT-006 (ocean_fraction): the slider minimum 0.31 is fixed but the real lower bound is 1 - 0.98 x (1 - shelf share): 0.304 at 0.29 and 0.608 at the shelf slider's top of 0.6 (SCL-021). The presets ship 0.7 and the hint says Earth 0.71.
- HNT-035 and HNT-034 (biome sliders): inland_interior's maximum of 20 000 blocks exceeds the 16k planet's circumference (16 384) and pole-to-pole distance (8 192); wetland_humidity's maximum 4.5 puts full strength at 6.0, above the scale's top of 5.
- Units and wording from arithmetic (HNT-073, HNT-074, HNT-075, HNT-002, HNT-077, HNT-027): river_width and river_depth carry the unit "blocks" but are blocks per sqrt(m³/s) and per (m³/s)^0.4; pet_per_degree "mm/°C" lacks "per year"; day_length says "Hours in one rotation" but the value is a solar day (SCL-024); lake_freeze says a mean of 0 °C gives ice for more than half the year (it is exactly half for a symmetric cycle, and the code tests strictly below 0); thrust_slip's "about 11 blocks" is the maximum of the coded slip law, not the typical slip. The block-conversion hints (53, 133, 13, 50 and 4 blocks; 7 blocks for 2 km; about 100 blocks for 250 km) are arithmetically right.
