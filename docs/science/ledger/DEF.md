Paths are relative to the repository root; line numbers are left out on purpose.

**Units in this ledger.** One block is 300 m across and 20 m up in the built-in presets, so the world is stretched 15 times vertically. Folds and faults are drawn at their shape in blocks and then X times taller, X = `true_height_share` x m_per_block_h / m_per_block_v (DEF-011; share 4/15 since 2026-10-02, X = 4 on the 20 m block), so an angle or an amplitude-to-wavelength ratio quoted below is at 1:1 in blocks unless it says otherwise; drawn, a dip d is atan(X tan d) in blocks and atan(share x tan d) in metres, and a vertical size is X times the 1:1 shape's in blocks and the share of the true-metre shape's.
