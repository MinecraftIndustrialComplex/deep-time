# Extraction notes: moisture, Köppen classes and biomes

Written by the stage-1 extraction agent for this subsystem (2026-10-01/02) while it built the ledger. It holds the measured Earth-against-mod numbers found in the build log, the simplifications and game choices the code or documents state, and flags: admissions of unchecked values, contradictions between comments, code and documents, and things that looked wrong. **Nothing here is verified.** A line marked "suspicion" or "my arithmetic" is the extraction agent's own reading, to be checked in stage 2. See [claims.md](../claims.md).
All paths relative to W. "Build log" = STATUS.md entry named. Observed data of the P1-26 design measurements: Beck et al. 2018 (Koppen) and Beck et al. 2023 (monthly T and P, 1961 to 1990), 0.5 degree, CC BY 4.0, used for validation only, brought to sea level "with the model's 6.5 degC/km on the same relief" (design doc section 1.1). Earth relief in those runs: GMT earth_relief_30m_p (SRTM15+).

## Earth against the mod

### The banded climate's measured faults (docs/design/p1-26-per-cell-climate.md, written 2026-09-30, the reason every MOI row is pending a rewrite)

Runs: (a) "real run": `deeptime stage climate --preset earthlike_quick_16k --start scattered_continents --length-myr 500` on master 424431b (L1 1024 x 512, 250 plate steps, 51 snapshots; ended at CO2 344 ppm, 19.7 percent of the land glaciated, Koppen land shares A 0.10 / B 0.20 / C 0.15 / D 0.15 / E 0.41; fields dumped with `deeptime dump-fields`). (b) "Earth relief": one snapshot of the same model at 720 x 360 on GMT earth_relief_30m_p, repeated six times with ice-sheet feedback as `climate-dem` does (EBM 15.26 degC, net TOA -7e-5 m2, 10.0 percent of land glaciated). Metrics are area-weighted; "along-row std" is the mean over rows at 30 to 70 degrees of the std along each row's land cells; eta^2 is the share of variance explained by 2-degree latitude bands.

Faults the document states in words (quoted):
- "latitude explains 99.6 % of the variance of land temperature at sea level. On Earth it explains 96.8 %. Along a latitude circle, winter land temperature varies by 3.1 K, against Earth's 6.1 K."
- "Sea ice is one value per band. Its southern edge varies by 0.23 degrees all round the planet."
- "The polar continent is uniformly ET or EF."
- "On Earth's relief the model gets: Yakutsk's January at -16 degC (observed -39 degC); Mumbai at 531 mm a year (observed 3,096 mm); the Sahara at 265 mm (observed 11 mm); Koppen agreement of 35 % exact and 65 % by main group."
- Causes in code (section 1.3): one temperature per band ("the same two numbers for the whole row, blended by a continentality between 0 and 1"); rain from a latitude table ("far inland the moisture march falls to its floor (moisture_floor 0.3), so interiors take the table's latitude profile: straight BW/BS stripes"); row marches ("Air never moves north-south, so a monsoon can only come from the ITCZ shift"); sea ice per band; per-row references between snapshots; satellite view blurs the bands; "Gyres are a prescription ... fixed +-3 to 6 degC offsets on coasts by basin position. They are not energy-conserving, they cannot move sea ice, and they are the only reason western Europe is mild."
- Maps ("looked at as quick-look PNGs"): January isotherms run straight east-west across Eurasia and North America on Earth's relief; the observed map has the cold pole over eastern Siberia, a warm western Europe and isotherms bending north-south along coasts; the real run has a uniform grey ET/EF polar continent, a "ruler-straight BWh stripe with straight BSh bands either side", and a staircase of 2-degree bands in the sea-ice edge. By aggregate Koppen metrics classes "are not much more banded than Earth's: eta^2 0.27 to 0.40 against 0.34"; what looks striped is the temperature-set boundaries (E/D at warmest month 10 degC, ET/EF, D/C at coldest month 0 degC, A/C).

## Simplifications and game choices

- **Earth-normalised planetary climate** (params.rs header; decisions file "Tectonics scaling"): lengths in Earth km shrink by R/R_earth (0.123 at 16k), so a continent of 60 degrees of longitude has the dry interior an Earth continent of that angular size has. Orographic rain and surface physics stay in real metres.
- **Banded temperature and rain backbone**: the EBM gives one temperature per latitude band; "per-cell effects only nudge them" (design doc). The belts' rain is a latitude table; "Gyres are a prescription".
- **Twelve equal months**, precipitation per month = annual rate / 12, a circular orbit for month timing and the belt shift; the rain's seasonality comes only from the shifting belts and the onshore term.
- **Rows marched independently** along the wind with a 300 Earth km blur ("the winds have meridional components and mix across rows"); air never moves north-south; a monsoon only arises from the ITCZ shift (design doc 1.3).
- **Linear orographic theory with a lee floor**: the lee's negative rate is applied proportionally and saturates at 0.3 of the background ("the linear theory's lee drying over-shoots on high terrain"); the rate is an event rate multiplied by 0.5 rain-hours per day; one FFT per wind regime over the rows it reaches.
- **Between snapshots** (every 10 Myr) the geographic anomalies move with the plates, so a rain shadow stays behind its drifting range; the zonal reference follows the fine clock's EBM.
- **Stand-in climate for presets with no climate section** (surface/climate.rs): "documented, not tuned"; "There is no rain shadow and no season".
- **Koppen**: monthly means only; summer half chosen as the warmer half-year (follows hemisphere and tilt); As/Aw split is "the common extension"; E tested before B (cold deserts are polar); no allowance for the 0 or -3 degC option of the research note.
- **Biome mapping is a placement, not a physical law**: every ramp, rule and band coordinate is a game choice by the P1-09 agent or Ben's decisions (all knobs made settings, 2026-09-29 "All of them"; the class table is a pack-overridable data file). Unsimplified facts: the band edges and distributions are vanilla's.
- **BW cells go to the hot band** (4.05 to 4.95 by annual mean 5 to 30 degC), so cold deserts become desert or badlands ("cold deserts included", build log P1-09); A and BSh never reach the hot band so "jungle stays jungle"; glaciated land capped at 0.35.
- **EF**: ice cap only where the glacier or ice-sheet model says ice (Ben, 2026-09-29), barren frozen ground elsewhere; humidity pinned (0.5 or 1.5) because P/PET is 10 below freezing; its erosion and weirdness are steered by data so Terralith's glacial chasm, scarlet mountains and cherry grove stay off (Ben, 2026-09-30).
- **Forced snow and ice cover** laid by Deep Time's own surface pass over glaciated ground whatever the biome (Ben, 2026-09-30, "Force snow/ice cover"): 160 m of ice per block, 12 blocks at most, 3 snow, blue ice at 9 or more. Reason given: Terralith has no flat inland ice biome (31 percent of ice-cap land had come out as gravel desert).
- **Lake ice** by the rule mean annual temperature below 0 degC (ice for more than half the year), not by coldest or warmest month; seasonal sea ice is not baked (Ben, 2026-10-01).
- **Climate API**: the climate (long-term monthly means) not the weather; lapse applied at the block's height but the Koppen class is the baked nearest-cell class; precipitation is not lapsed; below sea level the sea-level temperature.
- **The banded model stays until the per-cell model replaces it** (decisions log 2026-10-01: "the old banded model kept as a setting until the Phase 1 exit"; "Ice sheets on or off by default ... Keep holding").

## Flags

Suspicions are labelled SUSPICION and rest on my own recollection, not on a source in the repo.

1. **Koppen source chain is not what the header says.** koppen.rs cites "Peel, Finlayson & McMahon 2007 (Table 1; the rules the research note cites)". The research note (surface_processes_climate.md section 5) cites Wikipedia's Koppen article, with the B threshold written as "20 x mean annual temperature plus 280, 140 or 0" and "C and D split at a coldest month of 0 degC (or -3 degC)". Nothing in the repo says Peel's table was read. The coded thresholds look like Peel's to me (SUSPICION: recalled, not checked).
2. **-38 degC boundary.** Comment: d "if the coldest month < -38 degC". Code: `tcold > 0.0 || tcold >= -38.0` gives c, so exactly -38 is c. SUSPICION: Peel's table puts d at -38 or colder. The clause `tcold > 0.0 ||` is redundant (a leftover).
3. **0 versus -3 degC.** The research note offers both for the C/D split; code uses 0 (Peel's, if the recollection holds); the note's "-3" option is not implemented and is not mentioned in the code comment.
4. **E before B.** The comment says E is "checked first, so cold deserts are polar, not arid". Whether the published scheme orders the tests that way was not checked anywhere in scope.
5. **Summer half.** The code defines it as the warmer of April to September and October to March; the cited table (as I recall) defines it by hemisphere. On a planet with another tilt or an odd calendar the answers can differ; acknowledged in the header ("follows the hemisphere and any planet's tilt").
6. **Aridity thresholds attributed to UNEP.** biomes.rs and placement.json say 0.03, 0.2, 0.5, 1, 2, 5 follow "UNEP's hyper-arid, arid, semi-arid, dry sub-humid, humid". SUSPICION: UNEP's published boundaries are 0.05, 0.2, 0.5 and 0.65, and the index is defined with a Penman-type PET; the code's P/PET uses Thornthwaite PET "uncorrected for day length" (snapshot.rs), and the surface stage uses a third definition (60 mm/degC x T). The index feeds every humidity band.
7. **Two (three) PET laws in one pipeline.** Thornthwaite (aridity, so the biome humidity input); 60 mm/degC x T for runoff and the weathering kernel (surface/climate.rs, climate/mod.rs: kernel_of); hydro PET 60 mm/degC x temperature above freezing, said to be Holdridge's rule (PET = 58.93 x biotemperature; build log 2026-10-01 P1-22b follow-up) although 60 is used, not 58.93.
8. **Savanna humidity ceiling disagrees with its docs.** placement.json `savanna` squeeze is [1.05, 2.55] (and the test oracle's old constant is 2.55); the biomes.rs header and the P1-09 build log say "Aw/As and Cs squeezed into 1.05 ... 2.5". The coded Cs rule is 2.5. The docs are wrong for Aw/As, or the code drifted.
9. **Header table in biomes.rs is stale for EF and ET.** It states "ET/EF <= 1.95 humidity"; EF now uses fixed rules 0.5 and 1.5 (the header's own EF paragraph says so; the P1-09 build-log entry still lists "ET/EF <= 1.95").
10. **Lapse rate: 6.5 against 4.46.** Code uses 6.5 degC/km "the standard atmosphere's" (no document). The research note quotes 4.46 degC/km from Clima-Sim via Worldbuilding Pasta and does not say why it was not used. params validation allows 0 to 20; the editor 3 to 9. Separately surface/climate.rs has a second, fixed 0.0065 degC/m, independent of `climate.lapse_c_per_km`.
11. **Moisture reach: comment and default disagree.** lapse.rs says rain reaches "about 2,000 km inland" (the research note); the default `moisture_decay_km` is 3,500 Earth km, stretched by (1 + 1.5 x wetness) to up to 8,750 Earth km at the equator; the unit test uses 2,500. The build log P1-05 lists 3,500 as a model decision with no target.
12. **Gyre amplitudes are far below the note they cite.** The note says gyres warm western coasts by up to 15 degC and cool eastern coasts by 10 degC near 60 degrees; the code's amplitudes are 6/5 (mid-latitudes) and 3/5 (subtropics). No reason recorded. The design doc calls them "fixed +-3 to 6 degC offsets ... not energy-conserving".
13. **Orographic constants.** oro_cw 0.01295 = 7.4e-3 x 7/4: the 7/4 has no origin written. oro_nm 0.01 and the lapse ratio are said to be "fastscape-lem/orographic-precipitation defaults". SUSPICION: I recall the Python module's defaults being nm 0.005 and a lapse-rate ratio near 6.5/5.8, not 7/4; not checked against commit baf8a0c. NOTICE.md says the port took "the defaults of fastscape_ext.py".
14. **oro_hours_per_day 0.5** stands for "fastscape's rainfall_frequency" with no source for the value, no calibration run recorded in the build log; it sets the whole orographic scale (mm/yr = mm/h x 182.5). The measured Earth misses (Mumbai 531 against 3,096 mm; SE China 408 against 1,765 mm) sit where mountain rain and monsoon matter. The design doc plans to calibrate it.
15. **Two Clausius-Clapeyron rates for one law**: 0.07/K on the sea anomaly, 0.067/K (clamped 0.05 to 3.0) on the orographic term, and 0.02/K (Held & Soden) for the global cycle. The clamp is undocumented.
16. **Precipitation floor of 10 mm/yr** is written as a default; the polar plateau cells sit on it (2,744 EF cells at 10.5 mm or less, build log 2026-09-29 EF entry) and then become ice by the ELA rule with "no cutoff for dryness". Observed Sahara is 11 mm/yr (design doc), so the floor is at the scale of real deserts.
17. **Stand-in temperature claim.** surface/climate.rs: T = 28 - 42 sin^2(lat) is said to be "approximately Earth's zonal annual mean: 17 degC at 30, 7 at 45, -3 at 60". The formula gives 17.5, 7.0 and -3.5, and -14 degC at the pole. SUSPICION: Earth's polar annual means are colder than -14 degC in the south; this only matters without a climate stage.
18. **Belt shift uses a circular orbit.** belts.rs hemispheric_shift calls the circular `insolation::lambda_at`, while the EBM uses `Orbit::lambda_at` (Kepler timing) in the ice-age window; FORMAT.md says the months are equal 30-degree steps of solar longitude "(a circular orbit)". SUSPICION: a month-length mismatch of a few days inside the orbital window; low importance.
19. **Rain-share clipping.** bake/climate.rs stores each month's share as u8 / 40, so shares above 6.375 are clipped and the API then renormalises to 12: a strongly monsoonal month loses rain. The number of cells affected is not measured.
20. **API Koppen against height.** `ClimateSample.koppen()` is the baked class of the ground's cell; temperatures are lapsed to the block's height. A block high above the ground has a colder climate than its Koppen class implies; the Javadoc does not say so.
21. **"computed for every 16-block cell"** (DeepTimeClimate javadoc): the L1 cell size depends on the planet size (1024 x 512 cells at 16k blocks round); SUSPICION the figure is true for one planet size only.
22. **Admitted from-memory or unchecked values in scope.** (a) STATUS.md 2026-10-01 P1-22b follow-up, of the ELA at little accumulation: "close to the summer temperature at the equilibrium line that Ohmura et al. 1992 give for so little accumulation, about -2 degC; cited from memory, not re-read". (b) STATUS.md 2026-09-29 EF entry: the Ohmura "fit is extrapolated below its data (its lowest ELAs sit at a few hundred mm)". (c) docs/design/p1-26-per-cell-climate.md section 9: daggered references "Cited from memory, not re-read in this session": Ferreira et al. 2018; Lindzen & Nigam 1987; Bretherton, Peters & Back 2004; Manabe 1969; Klein & Hartmann 1993; Serreze, Kahl & Schnell 1992 (design only today). (d) plan section 7.2: the band boundaries come from OverworldBiomeBuilder, "this plan does not restate numbers it has not verified" (later checked by the GameTest `bandsAreTheBuilders`).
23. **Earth reference figures with no source**: "Earth ~ 8.5 degC / 715 mm" (build log P1-05), "Antarctic winter edge spans roughly 55 to 68 degrees S" (design doc 1.2), "Earth's zonal means in round numbers" (belt table), the Amazon recycling "a third to a half" (lapse.rs), glaciated land about 10 percent (the 15.8 of 149 M km2 split is in the build log without a source).
24. **EF/ET facts depend on one pack version.** BIO-074 rests on Terralith 2.6.2's parameter list and an offline emulation ("reproduces the real MIC run to 0.1 %"); the emulation scripts are "in the session scratchpad, not committed". A pack update can change the placement's effect without any code change.
25. **BiomeGameTests floors are a weak check by their own words**: "A floor that needs re-reading whenever the simulation changes is a weak test; it stays as a guard against a broken mapping only." They are re-set from the lowest share over ten planets less 0.1 (2026-10-01), after the same floors had been re-set on 2026-09-30 and on the first runs of 2026-09-29.
