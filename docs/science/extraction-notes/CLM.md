## Simplifications and game choices

Stated in the code or docs, with the reason where given.

- **Banded energy balance.** One temperature per 2 degree latitude band (90 bands) with separate land and sea surfaces weighted by the band's land fraction; atmospheric heat moves by diffusion of the band mean, ocean heat by diffusion through shared sea faces only (`ebm.rs` header; CLM-016 to 018). The design doc (section 1.3) lists this as the cause of banded results: latitude explains 99.6 % of land temperature variance against Earth's 96.8 %.
- **Circular orbit in deep time.** "the preset has no eccentricity" (`insolation.rs`); only the last 3 Myr get an eccentric, precessing, tilting orbit (CLM-031, 038). The series is "a simple analytic stand-in, not the true Earth solution" (`orbit.rs`).
- **Daily-mean insolation and a fixed 365.25-day year.** Planet day length does not enter the EBM (CLM-043).
- **No explicit clouds, no ocean dynamics, no salinity, no sea-ice drift** in the shipped EBM; the design doc lists the same items as "Not modelled" for the per-cell version too (section 2.6, Q9, Q10: no latent-heat feedback, "dry D includes latent transport").
- **Sea-ice cap.** Conduction through ice thicker than 10 m is tapered to zero "so no heat is invented or lost" and the latent store melts within model years; the comment says the real ice melts in centuries (CLM-096). Reason: the EBM runs only a few years per fine-clock step.
- **Ice sheets.** Thickness is Nye's plastic profile on an ELA-defined mask, capped at 3,000 m; "glacial cycles are ELA-driven (no ice-elevation feedback or ablation-zone flow)" so Earth-like swings are small (build log 2026-09-29 "For Ben" 1). Ice-sheet dynamics were approved 2026-09-30 and live on an unmerged branch.
- **Carbon cycle.** Only the silicate-weathering and degassing balance: carbonate weathering and burial "cancel over the long term and organic burial is left out" (`carbon.rs`). The atmosphere-ocean buffer uses an effective R = 5 (COPSE uses 1), which "changes only how fast CO2 moves, not where degassing and weathering balance".
- **Weathering without a supply limit.** Post-snowball drawdown can refreeze a low-degassing planet in about 1 Myr (build log "For Ben" 2); supply-limited weathering was approved 2026-09-30 and is on the unmerged branch.
- **Weathering runoff uses a stand-in PET (60 mm per degC)** taken at the temperature less the planet's global warming, so the thermostat is not defeated (`mod.rs: kernel_of`; bug 970efd1, build log 2026-09-30).
- **KERNEL_EARTH is measured on the model's own climate** so that Earth's relief weathers exactly Earth's degassing at 280 ppm (CLM-061).
- **Sea level = curve plus glacio-eustasy against a reference ice volume** of 1e7 km3 chosen to preserve calibrated coastlines, not Earth's ice (CLM-108; "alongside", decisions log 2026-09-29).
- **Event thresholds and magnitudes** (snowball 0.95 / 0.5 sea-ice share, hothouse 25 degC with 1 degC hysteresis, magnitude scales 50 Myr and 25 degC, cause thresholds 1.5 and 2.0, 5 Myr aftermath window) are implementation choices (CLM-084 to 090).
- **Numerics.** 72 steps per year, 40 and 6 years of EBM, early stop at 0.005 K, fine-clock steps of 100 kyr (2 kyr in the window; 200 kyr was approved for the unmerged branch), 10-Myr snapshots with anomalies carried with the plates in between (CLM-021 to 023, 081 to 083).
- **Snowball dust darkening removed** after trying (Abbot & Pierrehumbert 2010): "with Byrne & Goldblatt's forcing the ice melts on CO2 alone" (build log 2026-09-29).
- **Decisions behind features** (`docs/deeptime-design-decisions.md`): 2026-09-29 "Recent ice-age cycles", "Ice-linked sea level", "Greenhouse & sun over time", snowball Earth "emergent ... not scripted"; 2026-09-30 ice-sheet dynamics and supply-limited weathering ("Yes ... (Recommended)"), "200 kyrs, but make sure it is configurable"; 2026-10-01 P1-26 approved as recommended; 2026-10-01 present-day ice state of a generated world (a setting to prevent or force an ice age or snowball at the present; not found in the climate code read).

## Flags

Each flag is a fact from the text or a labelled suspicion. Suspicions are mine and come from the code and docs read here, not from a source.

1. **Planet gravity is ignored by the ice-sheet thickness.** `ice.rs: G = 9.81` ("Standard gravity") is used in Nye's profile while `PlanetParams.gravity_m_s2` exists and the preset carries 9.81. On a planet with another gravity ice thickness and glacio-eustasy would not follow (CLM-044, 100). Same for `RHO_ICE`, `RHO_SEA`.
2. **Obliquity comment against the code's own test.** `orbit.rs` says Earth's tilt swings between about 22.1 and 24.5 degrees with A_eps = 1.2 deg; the coded series has an additional 0.25 times 1.2 deg term at 53.6 kyr, and the unit test accepts a minimum between 21.5 and 22.6 and a maximum between 24.0 and 25.2 degrees. The series' real range is therefore wider than the comment states (CLM-032, 033).
3. **Earth's global mean temperature is quoted four ways, none sourced:** 15.6 degC (`ebm.rs`), 15 degC (`hydro_ref_c`, design doc target "15 +/- 0.5"), 14 to 15 degC (2026-10-01 P1-17 table), and the model's own 15.2 to 15.6 degC (EBM output on Earth relief). Also Earth land temperature 8.5 degC and precipitation 715 mm, glaciated land 10 %, all uncited (CLM-026, 078).
4. **Stale comment on the ice-sheet feedback.** `params.rs` says six 500-Myr pipeline runs end with "3-7 % of the land glaciated, 18 % on the coldest"; after the carbon cycle the 2026-09-30 build log measured 14.6 % and 25.4 % on the Earth-like runs ("the calibration's range was 3-18 %") and the 2026-10-01 table gives 0.09 to 0.23 per run. The calibration behind `ice_sheet_feedback` = 1 was made before the carbon cycle existed (CLM-025).
5. **Snowball sea-level fall does not meet the target the 3,000 m cap is justified by.** The cap comment says snowball ice sheets are thought to hold about 1 km of sea level (Hoffman et al. 2017) and that 4,000 m gave about 2 km; the build log's snowball scenario at 3,000 m still reaches -1.48 km, and "For Ben" item 3 says falls reach about 1.5 km (CLM-103).
6. **Escape CO2 and the forcing fit's range.** The snowball scenario escapes at 5.5e5 ppm (about 0.55 bar); the build log itself says Pierrehumbert's GCM needs more than 0.2 bar and Hoffman et al. 2017 quote 10^4 to 10^5 ppm. The Byrne and Goldblatt fit is stated as "fitted over 200-10,000 ppm" but the code applies it up to the validated 1e6 ppm bound, 50 to 100 times beyond that range (CLM-070, 071).
7. **Snowball threshold against the decision's wording.** The decisions log says "runaway past ~30 degree ice line"; the code's trigger is annual sea-ice cover of 0.95 of the ocean and the end 0.5, numbers that appear nowhere else. (Suspicion: an ice line at 30 degrees latitude covers about half of a sphere's area, so 0.95 marks a state well beyond the runaway point; it is a detection threshold, not the instability threshold.) (CLM-084, 085)
8. **Ice reference volume is not Earth's.** `ice_reference_km3` = 1e7 km3 against the model's own 2.47e7 km3 on Earth relief and about 3.0e7 km3 on Earth; the build log says "Earth's would raise present seas ~35 m" (item 5). Arithmetic on the comment: 3.0e7 km3 at the code's 2.47 m per 1e6 km3 is about 74 m, while the comment says 65 m for that volume (the 65 m figure presumably counts ice above flotation; the code's formula does not, suspicion) (CLM-106, 108).
9. **Present CO2 of the model's Earth-like runs is 210 to 220 ppm,** not 280 ppm, though `KERNEL_EARTH` and `CO2_REF_PPM` are normalised so Earth's relief balances at 280 ppm; the snowball scenario ends at 116 ppm. Measured values: 217 / 210 / 219 ppm (2026-09-29), 220 and 364 ppm (2026-09-30) (CLM-045, 061).
10. **From-memory and secondary-lookup admissions in scope.**
    - Design doc p1-26 section 9: "Cited from memory, not re-read in this session. The implementing tasks verify the page numbers before code headers cite them." for Ferreira et al. 2018, Lindzen & Nigam 1987, Bretherton, Peters & Back 2004, Manabe 1969, Klein & Hartmann 1993, Serreze, Kahl & Schnell 1992 (CLM-132, 134, 141, 143, 145).
    - STATUS 2026-10-01 P1-22b follow-up: Ohmura et al. 1992's summer temperature at the equilibrium line for little precipitation "cited from memory, not re-read" (hydrology agent; relates to CLM-114).
    - STATUS 2026-09-29 Deep-time climate: Byrne and Goldblatt's Table 2 "checked ... by a Sonnet lookup" (a secondary reading) (CLM-070).
    - Design doc: PlaHab "not inspected, nothing taken" (CLM-121).
11. **Ohmura fit is extrapolated and the constant is rounded three ways.** EF build log 2026-09-29: "the fit is extrapolated below its data (its lowest ELAs sit at a few hundred mm)"; the model glaciates 9,176 cells under 30 mm/yr by this rule. T_ELA at P = 0 appears as -2.4 (`glaciers.rs` comment), -2.35 (its unit test) and -2.3 (EF build log) (CLM-114).
12. **Hybrid of constants from two sources.** A, B, D are North, Cahalan & Coakley's but a0, a2, a_i, T_f and the 90 bands are climlab's, which were set to go with climlab's own A, B, D (210, 2, 0.555). The swap was made because the model "ran 3 degC cold". (Suspicion: the combination is tuned only by that global-mean outcome, not by a published joint calibration.) D is attributed to "North et al. 1981" in `params.rs` and to "North, Cahalan & Coakley 1981" in `ebm.rs` (CLM-003 to 009).
13. **Land heat capacity and ocean depth are uncited and tuned in the design prototype, not against a source.** `land_depth_m` = 3 m and `ocean_depth_m` = 50 m have no source in code; the design doc (section 2.3) keeps 3 m because the North, Mengel & Short 0.45 m value "fit worse" in the prototype (winter RMSE 8.6 K against 6.3 K). The sea's surface layer uses the same 3 m (CLM-013, 014, 122).
14. **Large-igneous-province spread.** `LIP_YEARS` = 1 Myr sits beside "Self et al. 2006" in the comment; whether that paper supports spreading a province's CO2 evenly over 1 Myr is not stated, and the 0.5 wt % CO2 and 2,900 kg/m3 are given without the paper's own numbers (CLM-057, 058).
15. **Weathering normalisation is circular by construction.** `KERNEL_EARTH` = 2.845e7 is measured on the model's own present climate, so Earth's CO2 at balance is 280 ppm regardless of whether that climate is right; the design doc (section 4.5) says it must be re-measured when the climate changes or "the carbon cycle's balance CO2 drifts" (CLM-061).
16. **Present-day perennial sea ice about 4.5 m thick** (`ebm.rs` comment; model measurement, no Earth figure given). (Suspicion, from memory: Arctic perennial ice is a few metres thick; not checked against a source here.) (CLM-096)
17. **Design doc header is stale.** `docs/design/p1-26-per-cell-climate.md` line 3 says "design for Ben's approval ... Nothing here is built yet"; STATUS row P1-26 says "Design approved 2026-10-01" and the decisions log has the 2026-10-01 approval. The doc also says the 200-kyr clock step is on the ice branch, while this code has 100 kyr (CLM-082).
18. **Preset description against the build log.** `earthlike_quick_16k.json` says "calibrated 2026-10-01, P1-17"; the 2026-10-01 P1-17 entry says "Not changed: ... the climate"; every `climate` key equals the Rust default (checked), so the preset adds no independent claim.
19. **Planet parameters the climate never reads:** gravity (CLM-044) and, inside the EBM, day length (CLM-043). Day length is used by wind code in `snapshot.rs` (other ledger).
20. **Units.** No inconsistency found in the constants read; `ice_reference_km3` and ice volumes are "Earth-normalized km3" and glacio-eustasy divides by Earth-normalized ocean area, consistent with `mod.rs`. D and the ocean diffusivity are in m2/K, as the EBM's flux form needs.
