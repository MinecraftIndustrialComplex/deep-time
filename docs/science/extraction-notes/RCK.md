## Simplifications and game choices

Stated by the code or docs, with the reason given:

- Temperature only, no pressure: no blueschist or eclogite is formed; no pressure-temperature-time history; the pool basement uses the present geotherm because pools have no burial history; both defer to P2-02 and plan Q8 (metamorphism.rs; STATUS 2026-09-29 P1-07 "Deviations (3)").
- Metamorphism acts on continental crust only: oceanic sediment is "subducted or accreted ... before it is buried deep enough".
- Contact metamorphism is a fixed outer shell (15 % of normalised radius) of the pluton body with no distance or temperature rule.
- Plutons: one body stands for an intrusive suite; batholith increments "merge at 300 m per block"; median size 25 km and rate 1e-5 chosen to emplace the same volume in a tenth as many bodies (P1-17 calibration).
- No sills are modelled: the structure features are fold trains, thrusts, normal and strike-slip faults, stocks, dykes and plugs (`Kind::ALL`); the batholiths of `plutons.rs` are ellipsoids, not sheets.
- Plutonic pools of the plate model are still stacked as flat layers in columns ("a composition ledger, not a structure"); the granite-type rock stays flat basement "until folds and faults" (decisions log 2026-10-01); bodies (batholiths, ore bodies) are painted where they are, so a fault younger than a body does not offset it (P2-01 stage B "Left open").
- The deposit engine's cover depth (`structure::Cover`) reads the undeformed stack (P2-01 stage B "Left open").
- Unconformities need no event; a structure is sealed by the first strata younger than itself; rock younger than a structure is not deformed by it.
- Sizes of folds, faults, dykes and plugs are "true in block space": amplitudes and slips are blocks times the shape's own ratio, not metres at the vertical scale, because the world is drawn 15 times taller than wide. Dips and slips of faults are therefore dips and slips as drawn (see flags).
- Dykes are a block or two wide ("Real dykes are metres wide; a block is the least that can be drawn"); fold amplitudes and fault slips are scaled by `strength`, counts by `density`, lengths by `size`.
- Rock layers are smoothed sideways (sigma 10 cells) so that dips are gentle; "the strata log itself differs a lot from one 16-block cell to the next" (build log 2026-09-29); the column rule is a B-spline blend so "a cell point shows a blend of its neighbours, not its own stored stack" (decisions for Ben, 2026-09-29).
- Every horizon at least one block, metamorphic at least 8, plutonic at least 16 blocks inside a stack; at most 32 horizons a cell (game constraints).
- The stone and deepslate stand-ins are chosen by look and commonness, not by depth: "the strata pass is unchanged: a class that resolves to stone is painted stone even where the crust was deepslate".
- Relief of structures on the ground fades over 10 Myr and is worn off by erosion at 5 times the structure's height; only land outside lakes moves, never below 1 m above the sea.
- Lava: only active volcanoes get a magma chamber and a conduit; crater lakes are 25 % of land volcanoes; vanilla's lava heights are kept as distances from the world floor.
- Dyke, stock and plug sizes and all rates are called "design values" in `deform/params.rs`.
- Noddy: not ported are DXF surfaces, imported blocks, Biot folds, alteration zones, geophysics, `unbury` and `unloft` (dead in the original); Deep Time adds a finite "window" to every ported event.

Game-choice rows were decided by Ben where the decisions log is cited (2026-09-29 continuous strata; 2026-09-30 settings and diamictite and coal cap; 2026-10-01 rock-order fix, stone for common rocks, folds and faults); the rest are agent implementation decisions recorded in the build log of the date given in each row.

## Flags

Each flag is an observation from the text, with the file; "suspicion" marks my own reading, not a checked fact.

1. Stock radius range contradicts its own definition. `deform/params.rs`: stock_radius_km comment says "By definition a stock crops out over less than 100 km² (a radius under 5.6 km); larger bodies are the batholiths", but the coded range is [4.0, 2.0, 10.0] clamped to 10 km, and the long half-axis is radius times 1 to 1.6 (`intrude.rs`), so some stocks are up to 16 km long and cover over 300 km2 (DEF-042, DEF-043).
2. Two different belt widths for the same Earth feature. `structure/params.rs` fold_belt_km = 300 km (traps) against `deform/params.rs` foreland_km = 250 km (strain), both compared with "the Zagros Simply Folded Belt is ~250 km wide; the Subandean belt 100-200 km" in the comments (STR-030, DEF-027). The 300 km default is above both quoted widths. STATUS.md 2026-10-01 P2-01 stage B: `fold_belt_km` "was mis-scaled", compared in block km, "eight times too wide on a 16k planet" until fixed.
3. Stale comments after P2-01. `structure/realism.rs` header: "Phase 1 has no thrusts and no folds (P2-01), so a column is a conformable pile cut by unconformities" while P2-01 stage B has landed and strata-check measures the structures. `structure/bake_horizons.rs` header: "P1-08 writes this layer and the bodies into the world data (this task produces the stacks for the viewer and the tests)". (The first two are the stale ones; `structure/columns.rs` and `structure/mod.rs` headers still read correctly.)
4. Class count in documents against the data. `docs/deeptime-design-decisions.md` row "MIC rock tags": "59 classes, 60 with `diamictite`"; `tools/dev/gen_rock_classes.py` and `lithology.rs` have 62 (impact_breccia and impact_melt added with P2-08, 2026-10-01); STATUS 2026-09-29 P1-07 says "The 59 JSONs".
5. Rock-class values were "reviewed" only against pictures. Every rock_class JSON says "set in P1-12; reviewed in P1-07 against the rock map and meridian cross-sections of pipeline_quick_16k and kept (STATUS.md, P1-07)": erodibility, diffusivity and density of all 62 classes carry that sentence. The sources named (Telford/Schon, Sklar & Dietrich, Stock & Montgomery, Roering et al., Heimsath et al., Hjulstrom) are cited for the ranking idea; the individual multipliers are "judgment, relative to granite". 41 of 62 densities have no source at all (RCK-002, RCK-004).
6. Hjulstrom 1935 cited for a stream-power multiplier. K_LOOSE (gen_rock_classes.py) cites Hjulstrom's curve (entrainment velocities of loose grains) to rank loose-class erodibility "about 4-10". Suspicion: that curve is about grain entrainment, while the coefficient is a bedrock-incision K multiplier; also the text says loose sediment is "ranked above all bedrock", but laterite is 4.0, equal to halite 4.0 (the top bedrock value) (RCK-008).
7. Fault dips cited from Anderson 1951 are applied in a vertically stretched space. `deform/params.rs`: normal dips 55-70 degrees and thrusts 20-40 degrees "the fault angles of Anderson 1951", but sizes and slips are drawn in block space with 300 m horizontal and 20 m vertical per block. The build log's own formula for real dip is atan(tan(block dip) / 15) (STATUS 2026-09-30 rock-layer settings), which turns 60 degrees into about 6.6 degrees and 30 degrees into about 2.2 degrees (derived). The text says this is intended ("What is kept is the shape a geologist would draw in a cross-section at 1:1"), but the cited angle is a real-world value (DEF-019, DEF-020, DEF-011). Same for fold amplitude over wavelength and slip over length: 52-block wavelength, 5.2-block amplitude is 15.6 km and 104 m in true metres (derived).
8. Slip ratios are above the cited value. Cited gamma about 0.03 (Schlische et al. 1996) against coded 0.05 (normal), 0.08 (thrust, times 0.5 to 1.0), 0.05 (strike-slip); the comment says they are "design values inside that spread, chosen so that offsets read at block scale" (DEF-016 to DEF-018).
9. Mixed unit conventions inside one subsystem. Plutons (`a_m = 500 * length_km`, thickness `b_m = 500 * thick_km`), metamorphism and strata thicknesses are true metres converted at 300 m horizontally and 20 m vertically (so they look 15 times taller than true); folds, faults, dykes and plugs are blocks in both directions. Both are documented, but a reader comparing a pluton and a stock sees two scalings. Also the workbench section tool reads "stretched 37× vertically" (STATUS 2026-10-01 Workbench rock at depth) while other entries say the exaggeration is 15×; the build log does not say what the 37 is relative to.
10. Areas on the block metric against Earth-km rates (suspicion). Pluton and structure rates are per km2 of `cell_area_km2` / block-metric area on a planet whose circumference is 16,384 blocks of 300 m (about 4,915 km; derived), about an eighth of Earth's. The pluton rate was derived from Earth's Coastal Batholith (1,600 x 60 km arc) and then applied to the plate model's arc flag area, which the volcano entry says covers "~12 % of the planet at 100k samples (bands a few samples wide plus broad hatched patches)". The same scale confusion was already found once for `fold_belt_km` (flag 2). Also the pluton count uses a cos(latitude) factor but the deformation emplacement explicitly does not ("a belt is as busy on the map at 60 degrees as at the equator"): two area conventions in the same stage (RCK-056, DEF-023).
11. The Coastal Batholith arithmetic is looser than written. `structure/params.rs`: "roughly thousand plutons in a 1,600 x 60 km arc over some 60 Myr (~1e-4 per km2 per Myr)": 1000 / (1600 x 60 x 60) = 1.7e-4 (derived), a factor of 1.7 above the quoted 1e-4; no source for the thousand plutons or the arc dimensions.
12. Citation granularity for the metamorphic temperatures. The seven formation temperatures cite "Winter 2010 ... ch. 21 and 25; Yardley 1989 ... ch. 3" at chapter level and "rounded"; plan §14 Q8 asks for "each boundary cited". Pressure plays no role, so the same temperature applies at any depth; gypsum to anhydrite at 50 degC is a single value (RCK-039 to RCK-045). No admission of recall appears in the text.
13. Implied maximum geothermal gradient. With the cap of 120 mW/m2 and k = 2.5 (m K) the gradient cannot exceed 48 degC/km (derived); the plate model's heat flow proxy "reaches 300 near ridges and arcs" and is clamped to 120, not scaled, so every cell above 120 mW/m2 gets the same gradient (RCK-030).
14. A pluton's roof depth is true metres but the pluton is evaluated against the cell's strata window at 2,000 m. `host_window_m` 2000 m is also the ore window; a pluton roof 2-6 km deep lies partly below it (the window comment says porphyry stocks sit within a few km), so the host-fraction for granite counts only plutons shallower than 2 km (STR-041, RCK-063). Suspicion only.
15. Preset against Rust defaults. The `structure` and `structure.deformation` sections of `earthlike_quick_16k.json` repeat the Rust defaults exactly (checked key by key); keys the preset omits (horizons, surface_temp_c, conductivity_w_mk, max_heat_flow_mw_m2, reference_crust_m, max_burial_factor, loose_depth_m, loose_age_myr, host_window_m, collision_pluton_factor, pluton_length_sigma, pluton_roof_km, aureole_shell, fold_belt_km, trap_min_sediment_m, salt_min_m, salt_overburden_m, growth_fault_sediment_m, and most deformation shape keys) take the Rust default. `structure/params.rs` says "plan §5.7.4: 32 in the built-in presets" for `horizons`, but no `horizons` key appears in the preset.
16. Superseded defaults still described elsewhere. STATUS.md 2026-09-29 P1-07 gives pluton rate 1e-4 and length median 10 km [3, 40]; P1-17 changed both. A reader of the P1-07 entry alone gets the old values.17. No source for several scientifically loaded design values with no "design value" label: collision_pluton_factor 0.3, the 70/20/10 granodiorite-granite-diorite split, the thrust and normal-fault strain weights (0.8, 0.6, 0.3, 0.4 for the foot wall), the 0.6 foreland strain weight, arc intensity 0.5 at 80 mm/yr, and the 80 mm/yr convergence scale itself (RCK-058, RCK-064, STR-036, STR-037, DEF-026, DEF-027).
18. `RISE` comment arithmetic is internally consistent (1 - 1/1.25^2 = 0.36) but the stated geometry "walls that are nearly upright" depends on the rooted ellipsoid's half-height being the ground height above the floor (hundreds of blocks) against a half-width of 1-2.5 blocks; STATUS 2026-10-01 notes "stocks are slender spires in section where the world is deep (13 blocks of radius under 400 of height)". Recorded by the author as left open.
19. Kimberlite, carbonatite and greisen are `special_host` family classes with no emplacement rule in this subsystem; the pluton and intrusion code never forms them (only granite, granodiorite, diorite, basalt, rhyolite, andesite). Their placement is elsewhere (deposits, ledgered by others).
20. Admissions of "from memory" or unchecked values: none found in this subsystem's code, data JSON, NOTICE.md or the build-log entries listed in the task (searched for "from memory", "recalled", "not checked", "could not confirm", "unverified", "abstract"). The text says "judgment" for 41 densities and every per-class erodibility and diffusivity value, and "design value" for most deformation defaults.
