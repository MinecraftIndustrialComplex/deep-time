## Simplifications and game choices

- **Anisotropic scale.** 300 m per block across and 20 m per block up, a vertical exaggeration of 15. Reason: Ben's answer 2026-09-27 ("a middle ground of about 1:300 ... customizable in both directions"; 20 m per block). The research report had recommended the same metres on both axes (8 m) so slopes stay true; consequences are managed with the drama slider, the glacial valley width (default 3, "1 = physical", decisions log 2026-09-30) and the volcano flank steepness (default 0.5, decisions log 2026-09-30).
- **Small planet, Earth-normalized tectonics.** The planet is 0.123 (16k) or 0.246 (32k) of Earth's radius. The plate model runs on a 6,370 km sphere with Cortial's constants and maps to the planet by latitude and longitude; surface physics (erosion, rivers, slopes) runs in real metres at map scale (decisions file "Tectonics scaling"; plan §8.1). Reason: Earth's plate sizes would not fit on the small planet.
- **Equirectangular map, not a sphere.** Rasters are equal-block per degree in both axes; surface processes run in block metric (isotropic), planet-scale inputs at true latitude, so continents stretch east-west by sec(latitude) (plan §5.2, `wrapped_planet_worlds.md` §5.3). Reason: the playable world is an X-periodic equirectangular grid (Toroidal World cylinder).
- **No seamless pole.** A soft cap from 75 degrees, hard stop at 80 degrees, ice-sheet ocean beyond the pole edge, pole transit "later" (decisions file "Poles"). Reason: the pole is topologically impossible on a flat block grid (a glide reflection).
- **Fixed build range -352..639 until 1.0** and terrain above the knee squeezed rather than cut or capped (decisions log 2026-09-28, 2026-10-01). Reason: the world height is a real limit of the game; Ben would rather raise the limit than "gimp the model". The bottom is not squeezed (open question, see Flags).
- **Planet constants beyond gravity do very little.** Gravity feeds the crust-strength height, volcano ceiling, impact scaling, diagenesis and the surface stage; day length only sets the climate's rotation rate (SCL-024); axial tilt and star flux act only in the climate. Tectonic regime is mobile lid only (decisions file "Space planets": v1 tunes only Earth-like presets). A build log (2026-10-01 The height limit, stage 2) admits that at 0.38 g fold belts are not markedly taller because erosion and uplift "do not know gravity".
- **Timeline.** No default story; 1 to 4,600 Myr; 2 Myr plate steps; keyframes every 50 Myr; surface frames and climate snapshots every 10 Myr (all agent defaults, plan §5.1/§6.1).
- **Resolution ladder** is "the starting point" (plan §5.2); the P1-17 benchmark was to replace it. L0 is an equal-area Fibonacci sphere.
- **Provinces.** View only; USGS-style classes; continental crust has no formation age so "whatever no orogen rule caught is taken to be old (Precambrian)"; island-arc specks under the map's 150 km feature size are not drawn; generalised like a printed map.
- **Bake provinces** (the ones that drive TerraBlender) are ten coarse settings from the plate regime, not the USGS classes; Ben said "Yes, for P1-10" (2026-09-29) to switching them; P1-10 is still `todo`.
- **Spawn** is chosen from dry, flat land by essentials and distance only.
- **Satellite view** is a stylised picture: colours, depth stops, snow and cover curves are the agent's choices to look right (Ben asked for "approximately what you would see on google earth", 2026-09-30); climate fields are box-filtered to hide model artefacts; hill shade uses 14x exaggeration on the model sphere.
- **Synthetic worlds** (`wrap-test`, `lake-test`) are test fixtures, with no geology.
- **Far terrain** has no physical model; it shows the generator's own height, water, ice cover and strata, without trees, caves or the pack's surface rules.
- **Quantisation.** Elevation is stored in sixteenths of a block (1.25 m); regolith thickness is capped at 255/16 blocks (319 m).

### Geothermal (GEO)

Hot springs, geysers and fumaroles are not implemented on this branch (planned as P2-14, "not started now"; decisions log 2026-10-01 says a geothermal branch exists separately). Geothermal gradients are used instead as follows:
- `crates/deeptime-core/src/surface/fields.rs`: the `burial_temp_c` field, `geotherm = heat_flow / 1000 / 2.5` degC per metre (conductivity 2.5 m/K as a literal), burial temperature = max(surface temperature, 0) + geotherm x source depth; consumed by the deposit engine (`deposits/fields.rs`).
- `crates/deeptime-core/src/structure/metamorphism.rs` and `structure/params.rs`: a linear geotherm T(z) = T_s + q z / k with `surface_temp_c` 10.0, `conductivity_w_mk` 2.5 ("a typical upper-crust value") and `max_heat_flow_mw_m2` 120.0 (heat flow above it is not used, "continental surface heat flow rarely exceeds ~120 outside active volcanic centres"; the plate model's proxy "reaches 300 near ridges and arcs"); `structure/mod.rs`: `surface_c`, `gradient_c_m` (heat clamped to [0, 120] / 1000 / 2.5); `structure/columns.rs` applies the present geotherm to continental crust for diagenesis and metamorphism.
- `crates/deeptime-core/src/climate/ebm.rs` comment: the geothermal flux, about 0.09 m^2, is what a very thick sea-ice cap's conduction balances.
These belong to the surface, structure and climate ledgers; none cites a source in the lines read here.

## Flags

**From-memory or unchecked admissions.** None of the files read for this subsystem say "from memory" or "could not confirm". Closest admissions (not about values): "A real client boot (the storm visuals, the F3 line, the client-side wind) stays unverified (plan §9.8)" and "Compiled only: no client was booted" (build log 2026-09-28 P1-18); the far-terrain look "stays unverified until a client with a GPU exists" (decisions log 2026-10-01); "the 28 km 'thinned' guess made half of it extended" (build log 2026-09-30 Geologic provinces: a first guess, since replaced by 20 km).

**Contradictions and stale text.**
1. Spawn rule against plan. Plan §7.8 says candidates are ranked by "temperate Koppen C or mild D, low slope, fresh water within reach"; `deposits/spawn.rs` ranks on dry flat land and the essentials only, and its module comment still says the climate and hydrology stages are pending ("The plan's other terms ... need the climate and hydrology stages (P1-05, Phase 2)") although both exist (BAK-016).
2. Bake note text after the squeeze. `bake/strata.rs`: `BuildLimit` docs say terrain above max_y "ends in a flat top at the limit" and `BuildLimit::note` still writes "rule: ... the game ends such ground at max_y", while `bake/terrain.rs` and the amplifier now squeeze everything below the knee so no cell should exceed it (BAK-004).
3. The `pole_cap_deg` key is dead on the Rust side. `ScaleParams.pole_cap_deg` (default 75, accepted in `SCALE_KEYS`) is read by nothing except validation; the Java generator reads its own `world_data.pole_cap_deg` and `pole_stop_deg` from `world_preset/planet.json` (and `planet_32k.json`), which is hard-coded 75 / 80. STATUS.md (P1-18) says the preset's value "reaches the generator when P1-11 writes world presets from a preset"; no code does so. `pole_stop_deg` has no Rust twin; the editor has no pole field. Likewise `circumference_blocks` is written twice (preset `scale` and `world_data`). A preset edit of the pole line would be silently ignored (POL-003, POL-004).
4. Provinces text drift. Build log 2026-09-30 Geologic provinces says extended crust includes the "passive-margin regime" and the filter is "3 x 3 majority"; after P1-27 the code uses a 1.35 degree band and radius 2 at 1024 columns (PRV-014, PRV-022). The `ramps.json` legend says basin is "or subsiding under over 1 km", omitting the 0.03 mm/yr condition in the code (PRV-017).
5. Satellite thresholds against its own text. Doc: herbs need a warmest month "over about 0 degC"; code ramps herb growth from -1 to 8 degC (half at 3.5). Doc: rainforest where the coldest month is "over about 18 degC"; code ramps 14 to 19 degC. Doc: "falls near UNEP's 0.2 and 0.5"; the stand-in 0.42 P/(20T+140) gives 0.21 at half the Koppen threshold and 0.42 at the whole threshold, not 0.5 (BAK-020, BAK-021, BAK-025).
6. `docs/research/notes/wrapped_planet_worlds.md` §5.3 uses 180/16,000 = 0.01125 degrees per block and "about 1,333 blocks" removed by the 75 degree caps at 32k; with C = 32,768 these are 0.01099 degrees and 1,365 blocks (the plan table has 0.01099) (POL-019).
7. Hill shade exaggeration 14 against the world's 15, and its slopes are taken on the 6,370 km model sphere while the game's map is 782 km (16k): slope there is not the slope a player sees (BAK-031).
8. Two scale bases for "km": `bake/meta.rs` computes the 50,000 km^2 minimum province in map kilometres (cell = r x 300 m), so it is 0.65 % of the 16k planet's 7.69 M km^2 (reader's arithmetic: about 3.3 M km^2 if the same share were applied to an Earth of 510 M km^2, a figure not in the repo), while `provinces.rs` works in degrees of the model sphere (1.35 degrees = 150 km Earth-normalized). The two province classifications therefore differ in scale as well as in classes (BAK-012, PRV-021).
9. Research report against the final choice. The report recommended the same metres per block on both axes at 8 m, stating this "keeps dips and slopes true" and that ocean depth would be clamped at about 127 blocks (1 km); the floor at -352 was justified as "3.3 km of crust mineable at 8 m". At 20 m the same floor is 8.3 km below sea; the justification no longer applies, and the 8 m reasoning about slopes is void under the 15x exaggeration (SCL-001, SCL-006).

**Units and scale that look inconsistent.**
10. Trenches below the floor. `tectonics.trench_elevation_m` is -10,000 m, which maps to y -437 at 20 m per block, 85 blocks below min_y -352 (plan §5.1 says "clamped to the floor band"). The six default runs have sea floor at y -412 to -472. Nothing squeezes the bottom; the stage-2 build log offers (A) min_y -512, (B) a symmetric squeeze, (C) leave it, and no answer is recorded (BAK-006).
11. The editor hint for `m_per_block_v` says "Below about 20 m the highest ranges no longer fit under the world's height limit", but the finished worlds peak at 6.0 to 6.9 km (6.9 km / 512 blocks = 13.5 m per block would still fit under the knee); 20 m is the fit for the plate model's 10 km guard, not for the measured peaks (SCL-020).
12. Maximum run length 4,600 Myr against `climate.present_age_gyr` 4.57 Gyr: a 4,600 Myr run would start 30 Myr before the star's birth (negative age in the brightening-sun law); `ClimateParams::validate` only bounds present_age_gyr to 0.5 to 12 (SCL-033, SCL-039). Suspicion: not tested by the reader.
13. `ocean_fraction` 0.7 in the presets against the editor hint "Earth: about 0.71"; the dry land target is therefore 0.30 and Earth's land fraction in the build log table is 0.289 (SCL-027). Not an error, but the 0.7 is not tied to a figure anywhere.
14. `LENGTH_MYR_RANGE` (Rust) and `MIN/MAX_LENGTH_MYR` (Java `Presets`) duplicate the 1 to 4600 range with no test linking them in the files read (SCL-033).
