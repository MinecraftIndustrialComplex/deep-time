## Simplifications and game choices

- USGS proxies instead of the named models. MVT uses OFR 2009-1252's CAam model (carbonate-hosted amagmatic, 132 deposits, 93 labelled MVT); SEDEX uses its SHam model (shale-hosted amagmatic, 25 deposits, all labelled SEDEX but only 25 of the 179 SEDEX-labelled deposits). BIF is one combined Superior plus Algoma model (Bulletin 1693 says they are not significantly different). Reason in the data files: the report has no MVT or SEDEX model, and Bulletin 1693 has no separate Algoma model.
- Many sizing inputs are placeholders and the type notes say so: ore densities for porphyry (2.7), MVT (2.9), SEDEX (3.2) and BIF (3.6); deposit densities for MVT (1 / 5 / 20), BIF (1 / 4 / 12), petroleum (5 / 20 / 160) and the unused child densities; BIF separation 5,000 m; formation depths for BIF, MVT, SEDEX, coal and petroleum; placer capture_fraction 0.5, transport_km 5, flatten gradient 5; supergene tonnage, leach depth and top depth; the coal rank bounds for lignite, subbituminous and low-volatile bituminous.
- Child types (supergene blanket and placer) take tonnage and grade from the parent, "not from sizing, which only documents the ranges" (supergene notes). Only copper is enriched by supergene processes (Mo, Au and Ag enrichment "has not been documented", SIR 2010-5070-B).
- Cu and Au (9 and 3 of 132) are left out of MVT and Au (4 of 25) out of SEDEX (type notes).
- Counts are of survivors, not of formed deposits: the USGS density is applied as the number surviving to the present within the assessment depth, and the engine forms extra deposits so the survivors match (decisions 2026-09-29 "Change the formula"); only the porphyry density is treated as a measured distribution (mean used), every other type uses p50 as an "estimate", never inflated.
- Province endowment (sigma 0 / 0.35 / 0.7 / 1.2) is a "design value, tuned by review"; the engine rescales so the planet's expected total is unchanged.
- The Thomas clustering camp spacings and radii, tract thresholds, zoning fractions, zone densities, lithocap and gossan sizes and aspect ratios are "design values" per the type notes.
- Playability: bodies are enlarged by size_mult_h and size_mult_v (porphyry 12 and 1, placer 12 and 10 so the ribbon is a block thick, MVT 12 and 3, SEDEX 12 and 4, BIF 6 and 4, petroleum 12 and 2, coal 1 and 20); coal gaps by spacing_mult_v 6; CDG sees 2 percent of an oil trap's physical charge (`cdg_scale`, "a gameplay value, judged", P1-16); essentials are regional minimums and the playability pass adds the smallest plausible deposit.
- The grade never reaches the blocks (audit 2026-10-01): block counts follow volume and zone density, not metal content.
- MVT driver: hot basin share and active-orogen head are derived from the history with design thresholds (aggrading 10 m per Myr, hot 50 to 100 C, active uplift 100 m per Myr, head smoothing 50 km); "the engine does not model salinity apart"; windows are octagons approximating the reach disk (within 5 percent).
- Bodies are ellipsoids or cylinders in the ledger's truncation formula, not real ore body shapes.
- A coal deposit is defined as a minable seam field (decision 2026-09-29), an estimate "not a USGS figure: no source counts minable seam fields".
- The deposit engine runs over a 500 Myr history, so very old types depend on inherited sheets (BIF takes formation_time cause).

## Flags

Status note: no row is `from-memory` because no text in this subsystem uses those words; the nearest admissions are quoted below.

1. Stale density comments. count.rs module doc: "ρ the type's deposit density (deposits per 100,000 km², its p50)". survival.rs module doc step 1: "ρ the type's P50 density". The coded rule (types.rs `Sizing::density_target`) uses the MEAN of the split lognormal for `density_basis: measured` (porphyry 17.8) and P50 only for estimates. The porphyry type notes contradict themselves: "the survivor formula takes its mean, 17.8 ..." and, in the same string, "so that the expected survivors are ρ_p50 · permissive area · count_mult". MVT, SEDEX and petroleum notes repeat the ρ_p50 sentence (correct for them). build log 2026-09-29 "survivor formula" also says ρ_P50 (superseded the same day by the mean-density entry).
2. coal.json stale note. data/usgs/coal.json tonnage_mt note: "SUPERSEDED in deposit_type/coal_seam.json (2026-09-29: a deposit is a seam field, 540 / 2,500 / 10,000 Mt, an estimate)". coal_seam.json now holds 3,800 / 19,800 / 115,000 Mt (its own notes: "rebased 2026-09-29 when a field became a stack of seams"). The data file still lists the 4.2 / 13 / 25 Mt mine-block placeholder, which no longer corresponds to the type.
3. coal_seam.json internal inconsistency on a page number: "coal deeper than 6,000 ft (1,800 m) is excluded from resources (printed p. 24, Table 3 p. 14)". coal.json says Table 3 is printed p. 24.
4. Research note against data and code for porphyry. ore_genesis_modeling.md Q2 and Q7 give Cu P10 "0.80%"; the data file and type use 0.75 and say "0.80 is only an axis tick on Fig. 3" (STATUS 2026-09-28: "the research note's 0.80 was a figure tick"). sizing.rs test fixtures still use 0.8 and ore density 2.6 (plan §8.6 also says 2.6 t/m3; the JSON says 2.7 placeholder). Research note Q7 example formation_depth_m 2500 and depth window 1000-6000 m; code uses 1500.
5. Porphyry density definition mismatch (suspicion, labelled as mine). The data file: densities are of "deposits AND prospects (286 counted, more than half uncovered) per 100,000 km2 of EXPOSED permissive rock in 19 well-explored control areas ... Density is inversely related to the exposed permissive area". The engine applies 17.8 (or 15) per 100,000 km2 to the whole permissive tract area, and the porphyry tracts it finds cover 40,000-82,000 km2 on the test landscapes (the source's control-area sizes are not recorded in the data file). The area regression of Singer et al. 2005 is not used (coefficients "not in the abstract"; only the abstract was read).
6. Circular 1178 citation cannot be checked from the repository. "Circular 1178 p. 15: USGS assessments count deposits to 1 km; a deposit counts if any part is within 1 km" (porphyry notes, plan §8.6, types.rs) appears in no data file and no source record; research note Q5 says "the USGS uses 1-3 km for assessments". The SEDEX density also rests on Circular 1178 tract data fetched by a Sonnet sub-agent with no recorded read statement.
7. formation_depth_m ambiguity (suspicion). types.rs defines it as the depth of the body's centre when it formed; the 1500 m for porphyry is "the median emplacement depth of the Cu subtype (OFR 2008-1155 Fig. 24, digitised)", subtype 17 only (Cu-Au 1.2 km, Cu-Mo 2.3 km in the data file), and the source says depth below the paleosurface without saying top or centre. Also vertical_extent_m 1250 is the midpoint of "1 to 1.5 km" and is not stated.
8. Placers from every eroded porphyry (suspicion). porphyry preservation.erosion_behavior is spawn_placer; the data file records that only 4.1-9.5 percent of porphyry deposits have a placer within 10 km (OFR 2008-1155 Fig. 23, digitised) and that the figure's denominator is unknown. capture_fraction 0.5 is a placeholder ("no quantitative rule ... was found").
9. Supergene precipitation bound against the data file. Code: precip_mm [100, 1500] from "Precipitation ... inferred to be 10 cm/year or more" (central Andes, maximum enrichment). The data file's own note (precipitation_cm_per_yr_min): "supergene enrichment also occurs from tropical to arid climates (Titley, 1978), and even in the hyperarid Atacama; the critical factor is permeability." The code excludes dry climates the source says can enrich.
10. Supergene time and factor provenance. min_stable_myr 0.5 is attributed to Sillitoe 2005; the data file: "The research note's 'typically 3-9 Myr' (attributed to Sillitoe, 2005) was not found in any USGS document opened"; USGS gives minimum durations of 0.4-6.2 Myr per deposit (Sillitoe & McKee 1996, abstract). grade_mult [2.0, 3.0]: USGS says "up to 3 times" in the Andes and "approach 8" in SW North America; the lower bound 2.0 appears only in the research note ("2-3x hypogene").
11. BIF grade basis. data file note: "The 53% Fe median is far above the 30-35% typical of primary iron formation ... This compiler infers that many of the 66 entries are enriched (supergene / direct-shipping) ores; Bulletin 1693 does not say so." The mod uses 53 percent as the grade of every BIF sheet in a host that is primary iron formation.
12. BIF density wording. data file: "Mineral specific gravities (hematite ~5.3, magnetite ~5.2) are textbook facts, not taken from a USGS document here"; the coded 3.6 t per m3 is a placeholder with no stated derivation.
13. Porphyry ore density wording. data file: "PLACEHOLDER, NOT FROM USGS ... 2.7 t/m3 is a generic density for fresh intermediate-to-felsic intrusive rock ... from general geological knowledge" (the nearest admission to "from memory").
14. Proxy distributions differ from the named models (data files extra). MVT: CAam P10 86 Mt against the MVT-only SIR 2010-5070-A Table 1 P10 (its 90th column) 42.4 Mt, median 10 against 7.0. SEDEX: SHam 5 / 25 / 300 Mt against Bulletin 1693 m31a 1.7 / 15 / 130 Mt; SIR 2010-5070-N paraphrases m31a as "between 1.4 and 120 Mt; the median deposit size is 15 Mt" while the printed intercepts are 1.7 and 130. Cu-Mo porphyry subtype P10 4,800 Mt may be a typo for about 3,800 (data file note; not used by the engine).
15. SEDEX density arithmetic. type notes: "Percentiles do not add, and known deposits are not in those counts, so this is an estimate ... and a lower bound". The 2 / 10 / 26 sums over 207,508 km2 are sums of tract percentiles, which the notes accept as 1 / 5 / 12.5 per 100,000 km2. The sources cited for these tract numbers (Circular 1178, mrdata nmra) are not recorded with page numbers in the data file.
16. Oil density inconsistency (units). petroleum_trap.json converts MMBO to tonnes at 0.1364 t per barrel (stated as 0.858 t per m3), while ore_density_t_m3 0.13 and the CDG charge use 0.85 t per m3 (mod.rs OIL_T_M3, a file outside my scope). About 1 percent apart. The field-size distribution is one province's (Niger Delta Agbada) undiscovered oil fields used for every petroleum trap on the planet ("One province's distribution, not a global model"); the unit is oil volume, while the dome is sized in reservoir rock volume at 0.13 t per m3.
17. Petroleum "80 C" threshold has no citation in the type (type notes: "a layer generates only while hotter than 80 °C (petroleum.rs)"). The data file quotes an 80-120 C oil window from OFR 2003-253 p. 8 but the Waples TTI scheme does not itself state 80 C; ~50-150 C catagenesis was "NOT found stated in a USGS document opened".
18. TTI sources. Waples 1980 values were "taken from the abstract as indexed by the U.S. DOE OSTI record 6845191"; the TTI-to-Ro table is Kansas Geological Survey (non-USGS) after Waples 1981 and "Waples' 1980 TTI-Ro table itself was not opened". The coal rank bounds (lignite 3, subbituminous 10, HVB 75, LVB 500) are "PLACEHOLDERS (no USGS table of reflectance by rank was found)"; anthracite's upper bound is the sentinel 1,000,000,000.
19. Host weights make non-hosts the best hosts (suspicion). types.rs `Hosts::weights`: "a class listed only [in host_bias] becomes a host with that weight". Porphyry host_bias serpentinite 1.5 adds serpentinite as a host weighted above granodiorite and granite (1.0) although porphyry deposits are not described as serpentinite-hosted; SEDEX host_bias dolostone 1.5 adds dolostone above black shale (1.0) although the type describes euxinic black shale (the data file does say 72 percent dolomite index for carbonate-hosted SEDEX). 20. Window mismatch in MVT notes. The carbonate_platform curve note says "a fifth of the column's top kilometre counts fully" while hosts.full_at 0.05 is "about 100 m of carbonate in a 2 km window" (structure.host_window_m, outside my scope): two different column windows are named for the same host test.
