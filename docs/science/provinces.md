# Geologic provinces

Status of this page: `stub` (stage 1). The ledger rows exist; the page is to be written in stage 2 from [_template.md](_template.md).

A geologic province is a region with one kind of geologic history: an old stable shield, a platform of flat sediments over it, a mountain belt (orogen), a sedimentary basin, a large igneous province, stretched crust, or ocean floor. The mod classes every cell of every keyframe this way, after the classes of the USGS map "Geologic provinces of the world", for the map view and, later, to steer biome regions.
